If your Canton Dev Fund proposal failed, it probably was not rejected.

Seven votes have failed. Six recorded no vote against at all. They ran out of
time while the eligible voters did not vote, and the threshold is 51% of
allowed voters, so not voting counts the same as voting no.

Four of those seven were re-opened within days. A failed vote is a restarted
clock, not a verdict.

Disclosure: proposal #860 is mine and no vote has been opened on it. Which is
why every number comes from a tool you can run yourself.
