"Security Advisory for Validator and Super Validator Operators"
Canton Network forum, Announcements (category 56), topic 9421
Author: pedrodneves. Created 2026-10-26T17:22:58Z
https://forum.canton.network/t/9421

We are in a period of heightened security relevant activity in the industry in general and against that backdrop also within Canton Network.

The predominant attack vector we've observed in recent times is that attackers target validator operators' internal networks or infrastructure to gain access to validator deployments.

For nodes that hold their namespace root keys locally and/or hold significant amounts of Canton Coin in wallets by node-internal keys, this can allow attackers to take control of the node and/or the Canton Coin in those wallets. Please stay vigilant as these types of attacks are being attempted in practice.

Have your incident response plans ready. When in doubt: Turn off your validator / Reach out to security@canton.foundation. The foundation can help in various ways including by coordinating with the CEXs blocks those funds from leaving through them.

If you suspect your node's root namespace key was compromised: Start monitoring the network for transactions signed with the compromised key. Un-host all parties (internal and external) from your validator (send topology transactions through a non-compromised node). Move the parties owned by the compromised root key to a non-compromised validator. If the root key was compromised, you will have to race against the attacker.

Review where credentials are being stored (wikis, docker images, local laptops, etc) and consider moving to secure vault.

The Canton and Splice teams are working on publishing runbooks to upgrade existing node deployments to convert to KMS-based key management.
