**Disclosure first: proposal #860 is mine and no vote has ever been opened on
it. Every number below comes from a tool anyone can run.**

I went looking for why some proposals sit still, and found something more
useful than I expected.

Seven votes have failed. **Six recorded no vote against at all.** They ran
out of their 21 days while the eligible voters did not vote. The threshold is
51% of allowed voters rather than of votes cast, so an absence counts exactly
like a no.

**Four of those seven were re-opened within days of expiring.** #813 the same
day, #866 and #453 on 9 November, #577 on 10 November. So a failed vote is a
restarted clock, not a verdict, and reading your own failure as a rejection
of the work is probably wrong.

All seven were opened within ninety seconds of each other on 16 October, so
all seven expired on the same day. One batch, not seven decisions.

One thing for anyone measuring this themselves: the repository's labels show
**three** failures, not seven. A re-opened vote has its label reset to
`gitvote/open`, so the first failure stops being visible. My own tool counted
that way and was wrong by four until I checked the vote comments instead.
