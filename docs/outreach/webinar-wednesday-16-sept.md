## What the session is actually about, so the question fits the room

BitSafe's product is **DecMan**. Its pitch: *"privacy alone doesn't keep a
network running when a node fails."* It lets asset issuers keep Canton's
privacy while spreading operational risk across approved node operators.

Three areas they will cover:

- **High availability**, so an application survives an operator outage
- **Shared custody**, MPC wallets and token vaults with no single party in
  control of the assets
- **Administration**, decentralised governance

Their framing line is *"privacy and resilience are separate problems"*. There
is a live Q&A.

## The question

One question. Type it, then stop typing. Both replies that have worked for us
were one question, and the last time we asked two the person answered neither.

```
With shared custody, what happens to an action that does not reach
quorum? Is there a record of the attempt? Or only of the ones that got
signed?
```

That is a real question and we do not know the answer. It sits inside their
own vocabulary, it is specific to DecMan rather than generic, and it does not
mention us at all.

It also happens to be our problem in their domain. An action that fails to
reach quorum is an action that was refused, and on most designs it leaves
nothing behind. If he has solved it, we learn something. If he has not, the
room hears the gap from a question rather than a pitch.

**Do not follow up in the chat.** If he answers well, that is the opening for a
message afterwards.

## If the quorum question does not fit the flow

Their own framing gives a second one, shorter:

```
You said privacy and resilience are separate problems. Where does
auditability sit, given a blocked action leaves nothing on the ledger?
```

Use one or the other. Never both.
