# Five firms, one question, one afternoon

**The question, and it is the same every time:**

> Do you ever subcontract Daml work when a client lands?

It is not a favour and not a job application. A firm with more work than people
says yes. One without says no. Neither costs you anything, which is why five
asks is one afternoon rather than five ordeals.

**Send all five. Not one and then wait.** One reply in five is a normal rate.
Sending to one and waiting is how a month goes.

---

## The message

Same body for all five. Change only the first line.

```
Do you ever subcontract Daml work when a client lands?

I ask because of what I have shipped, not what I would like to do.

Three pull requests merged into the Canton Developer Hub. Eight fixes to
the Cantor8 hackathon toolkit, four issues and four pull requests. A Daml
package, canton-refusal-record, with 111 test scripts, where deleting any
spending rule turns a named test red. A Dev Fund proposal open under RFP
27.

github.com/dominicrume/cantor8_kya_rails

Available now, contract or fixed scope, UK based and remote.

If it is not something you do, no reply needed.
```

**Why it is shaped like that.** The question first, so they know in one line
what is being asked. Shipped work, never ambition. Availability in one line.
The last line means silence is a complete answer, so nothing is owed either
way and nobody has to manage you.

## What not to do

- **Do not attach the CV.** They asked for nothing. The link is enough, and a
  link they choose to open beats an attachment they did not.
- **Do not mention the Dev Fund proposal as leverage.** It is one line of
  evidence, not a reason for them to act.
- **Do not follow up inside two weeks.** These are firms, not friends. Silence
  at two weeks is a no, and a second message makes it a definite one.

## If one says yes

The rate to hold is **£675 a day**, which is the Central London median for DAML
contract work against £438 for developers generally. It is a scarce skill and
the number is public. Do not open below it.
