## Why this wording

The PRs come first because they are the thing being offered, and they are
theirs, not ours. The secret is second and is one sentence. The reason is one
sentence and is specific, so it is obvious the request is not idle.

Nothing here asks them to hurry, and nothing complains that four pull requests
have sat for three weeks. THE-RULES: an update is welcome, a debt collector is
not.
